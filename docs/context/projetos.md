## 6. Midnight Meow

- **Sinopse:** "Have you ever wondered what your cat gets up to at night? In Midnight Meow, you play as a kitten venturing out to defend your home from evil rats."
- **Gênero (One Page):** tower defense, shooter, com elementos de pinball; mecânica principal: arremessar o novelo de lã e recuperá-lo.
- **Contexto:** Game Jam Plus 2025/2026 — **Melhor Pitch**. Equipe (Notion): "2 programmers, 1 artist, 1 game designer, and 2 sound designers". Equipe (One Page): Debs Carvalho, Juliana Acunha, Lucas Gonçalves, Luiz Henrique, Matheus Silva, Pedro Caurio, Vitor Emanoel.
- **Papel:** "Soundtrack Production". Além disso: "I produced the Menu, Credits, and Game Over themes. For the Game Over track, I aimed to avoid a punishing tone, ensuring the player wouldn't feel discouraged."
- **Destaque técnico (trilha adaptativa):** "In the game, enemy waves are audio-driven. They spawn as the music's tension increases. To achieve this, I divided the soundtrack into several distinct tension levels."
- **Intenção sonora:** "I wanted the soundtrack to do justice to Nyxie, our grumpy-looking protagonist. Additionally, since the enemies are rats, I incorporated sounds reminiscent of them into the track."
- **Mídia:** teaser YouTube `ugLLJmAIxQo` (Notion) e `-G5KOAxw3FQ` (canal); pitch `xuEVauS7qNk`; 8 stems de tensão + menu/créditos/game over no Drive; playlist SoundCloud `midnight-meow`; One Page em PDF.
- **Link:** https://midnight-meow.itch.io/midnight-meow

## 9. Feed The Cats / Alimente os Gatos

- **Sinopse:** "Feed the Cats is an FPS game about feeding kittens. The game follows a professional cat feeder who needs to feed them in a snack factory."
- **Foco:** "create an FPS game in a 3D map but with 2D assets. Instead of following the standard shooter game formula, I decided to work with a cuter aesthetic, making a game about feeding kittens with a snack launcher."
- **Papel:** projeto **solo** — "Audio Design, 3D modeling, 2D art, UI design, scriptwriting, and programming. [...] Although I didn't compose the soundtrack or create the sound effects, I was responsible for their curation, mixing, and implementation."
- **Intenção sonora:** "I wanted the treat launcher to sound soft and non-aggressive. I chose a tennis ball machine sound to keep it lighter and less harsh than a realistic weapon." / "I also didn't want the cat sounds to sound aggressive. I wanted them to be soft, to show they're happy about getting treats."
- **Mídia:** teaser YouTube `n8RU80fHyfY`; 5 SFX (tiro de bola de tênis, sem munição, pegar sachê, miados); 3 screenshots; GDD em PDF.
- **Link:** https://ju-acunha.itch.io/alimente-os-gatos
